# 0025. Sign-in: GitHub, Google, email and password

Status: accepted (2026-10-11) · Spec: [risks.md](../risks.md)

## Decision

GitHub, Google, and email and password at launch; providers are pluggable.

## Why

Covers most users; single sign-on for teams can be added later.

## Consequences

Local use requires no sign-in at all.
