# 0026. Hub pricing: free up to 3 machines

Status: accepted (2026-10-11) · Spec: [risks.md](../risks.md)

## Decision

The hosted hub is free for up to 3 machines, then a subscription. The local product is free; users always bring their own agent logins.

## Why

dent never resells model access.

## Consequences

The price above the free tier is still open.
